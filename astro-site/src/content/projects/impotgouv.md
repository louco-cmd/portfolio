context: >
  France is known for its complex tax system — but does any country truly approach
  tax declaration with a UX perspective? This project stems from a simple question:
  why do we need tax consultants just to understand how to file taxes? Paying taxes
  is a civic duty everyone should be able to fulfil with ease, so why is it such a
  painful experience? The aim is to make the tax system more user-friendly — perhaps
  even enjoyable — and maybe cure the widespread "administrative phobia".
objectives:
  - Leverage UX best practices (e.g. progressive disclosure) for a smoother, more intuitive experience.
  - Integrate cutting-edge technology such as AI-powered LLMs specialized in tax queries.
  - Adopt the latest French government design system for consistency and accessibility.

context: >
  Paris Escrime Sporting Club is one of the many fencing clubs in France's capital
  and perhaps one of the youngest. Over the past five years, it has grown
  significantly thanks to effective event planning and the increased visibility of
  fencing due to the Olympic Games. However, the club's current website is failing
  to meet its purpose, creating extra work and frustration for the owner due to:
objectives:
  - A high volume of unnecessary inquiries caused by unclear information.
  - A confusing class booking system.
  - A weak online communication strategy, leading to missed opportunities.

context: >
  While living in China, I needed to learn Chinese and keep track of my own
  progress — but no existing tool really fit the way I wanted to learn. So I
  built my own. It grew into a social platform I play on with my friends, and
  then into a company that went on to win the Entrepreneur award of the
  CCI France Chine (the French-Chinese Chamber of Commerce). Jiayou is a free Chinese-learning
  app for iOS, Android and the web: capture any word you meet, master it with
  smart quizzes, challenge friends in duels, and learn together through a
  growing community — all in one pocket-sized app.
demand: >

  apps get wrong: a vocabulary experience that is personalized, genuinely fun to
  open, and social — teaching "the Chinese people actually speak" rather than
  textbook phrasings nobody uses.
objectives:
  - Let learners capture every new word, in English or Chinese, in seconds.
  - Turn passive vocabulary into mastery through personalized, HSK-aware quizzes.
  - Make practice social and motivating with duels and a shared community.
